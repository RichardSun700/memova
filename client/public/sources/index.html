Apollo demonstration

Sources & disclosures

The Apollo story is an illustrative product scenario: it imagines how a member of the Apollo 11-era team might have used Memova to connect records, create a Personal Manual, and prepare actions.

Historical reconstruction — not an endorsement

Memova did not exist during the Apollo program. The Personal Manual, HTML Pages, emails, calendars, social previews, and product interfaces shown on this website are illustrative reconstructions. They were not authored, approved, or endorsed by Neil Armstrong, his estate, any Apollo astronaut, NASA, or SpaceX. Memova is not affiliated with NASA or SpaceX.

How archival imagery is used

NASA is credited as the source for NASA archival material. Photographs may be cropped, color treated, composited, or separated from their original backgrounds for an informational product demonstration. The website does not present those edits as original historical frames, does not use NASA marks to imply approval, and does not treat any identifiable astronaut as a Memova customer or spokesperson. See NASA's current Images and Media Usage Guidelines.

How to read the demo

  • Historical photographs and mission records are used as source context; Memova's product interfaces and outputs are modern illustrative reconstructions.
  • Cutouts, color treatment, compositing, and some decorative visuals may be digitally or AI assisted. Editing does not change the attribution of the underlying source.
  • Social-media screens are illustrative channel previews, not real accounts, posts, verification status, or platform partnerships.
  • Names and marks belonging to NASA, SpaceX, and third-party platforms identify historical subjects or example destinations only; no sponsorship or affiliation is implied.
  • When an image-specific identifier is available, it is listed above. General Apollo background and mission context links point to the applicable NASA mission or media library.
