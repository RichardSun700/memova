For most of human history, information was power.在人类历史的大部分时间里，信息就是力量。

The people who knew more had the advantage.

Knowledge was limited, access was difficult, and discovering new information created an edge.

But AI is changing that.

知道更多的人拥有优势。

知识有限，获取困难，而发现新的信息意味着竞争优势。

但 AI 正在改变这一切。

Information is no longer scarce.

信息不再稀缺。

Answers are no longer difficult to find.

答案不再难以寻找。

Ideas can be generated in seconds.

想法可以在几秒钟内被生成。

We are entering a world of abundance.

A world where anyone can create a story, an image, a product, or an idea with the help of AI.

我们正在进入一个“过剩”的时代。

一个任何人都可以借助 AI 创造故事、图片、产品和想法的时代。

But when creation becomes unlimited, a new problem emerges:

What deserves our attention?

但当创造变得无限时，一个新的问题出现了：

什么值得被关注？

The challenge of the future is no longer producing more.

未来的挑战不再是创造更多。

It is choosing better.

Because not everything that can be created should exist.

因为并不是所有能够被创造出来的东西，都值得存在。

AI can generate thousands of possibilities.

AI 可以生成成千上万种可能。

But humans decide which ones are worth pursuing.

但人类决定哪些值得被追寻。

AI can create endless content.

AI 可以创造无限的内容。

But humans decide what is meaningful.

但人类决定什么拥有意义。

A machine can tell us what is popular.

机器可以告诉我们什么正在流行。

But only humans can decide what is important.

但只有人类能决定什么真正重要。

The most valuable human skill in the AI era may not be intelligence.

在 AI 时代，人类最珍贵的能力也许不再是聪明。

It may be taste.

而是品味。

  1. The ability to recognize beauty.

    发现美的能力。

  2. The ability to find meaning.

    寻找意义的能力。

  3. The ability to choose what is worth remembering.

    选择什么值得被记住的能力。

The future will not belong to those who create the most.

It will belong to those who know what truly matters.

未来不会属于创造最多的人。

而会属于那些知道什么真正重要的人。
